Engineering Philosophy
I believe engineering is about more than producing a finished product. It involves understanding a problem, exploring possible solutions, testing ideas, learning from failures, and refining designs through iteration.

Across my robotics projects and research, I aim to document not only what I build, but also the reasoning, experimentation, and technical decisions behind it.

About This Portfolio
This portfolio brings together my work in competitive robotics, embedded systems, electronics, and automotive engineering research. Each project serves as an opportunity to develop technical skills, explore engineering concepts, and document my progress as a student and aspiring engineer.

This portfolio will continue to evolve as I develop new projects and expand my engineering experience.
